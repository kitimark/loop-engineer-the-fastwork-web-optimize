Each best build replaced the one before it only after a phone check: a phone no-worse check, a blind swipe or your own OK. On 7 Oct you asked for the original end-of-page motion back, and the g0 line started from v14 with that end restored.

Who worked

The agent team

How the roles worked together

  • Fix owners took one problem each, in their own copy of the repo, and measured it under the shared Chrome lock.
  • Toolsmiths fixed one tool bug (an "incident") each, test first. They were the largest group, because the tools grew with every problem.
  • Reviewers checked finished fixes adversarially. They sent several fixes back before landing.
  • Design engineers and architects worked out the rendering maths, the measurement protocol and the GPU playbook.
  • Management (mgmt-ops, desks, steward) took routing, digests and device watching off the lead after you called the lead a bottleneck on 4 Oct.
  • Mappers and helpers did read-only lookups; on 9 Oct they wrote this summary, one helper per stretch of days.
