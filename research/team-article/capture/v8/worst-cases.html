Worst cases on the original

You asked where the original page lagged most. We went back through every recording we have of the original. The ranking is by the longest frames and by how many slow frames came with them. Most of the worst cases are on the iPhone, and several need a finger or a hot phone, so we can only describe them. Chrome can replay two of them: fast flicks, and flicks the moment the page has loaded. Those two are the clips below. All numbers are OBSERVED in our setup. A frame over 50 ms is one the eye can catch as a stutter.
