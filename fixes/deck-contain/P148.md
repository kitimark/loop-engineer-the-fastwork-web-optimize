## The instrument (changes from P-146)

1. **Motion hold** (`px148-gate.js`, loaded by `<script src="/px148-gate.js">` as the first child of `<head>`, so it
   runs before every page module):
   - while held, page callbacks of `window.setTimeout`, `window.setInterval` and `window.requestAnimationFrame` do not run;
   - held timeouts and frames are queued and run when the hold ends, and interval ticks are skipped;
   - the instrument's own frame waits use the original `requestAnimationFrame` (`__dcRAF`);
   - the hold is identical for base, arm and base-again, and it is off outside a triple.
2. **Instrument overlay** `fixes/deck-contain--px148/overlay` (untracked, never committed), built by
   `mk-px148-overlay.mjs`:
   - the v9.1d base overlay files, copied byte for byte (hashes `941aa13e5bdf`, `f1997edf5309`, re-checked and recorded
     by every driver run);
   - `px148-gate.js`;
   - the baseline `index.html` with the one script tag (`c16c1e8abb22`).
   - Served by `fix.mjs serve deck-contain--px148` (Chrome) and by `serve.mjs --overlay` (Simulator, as in P-146).
3. **Triple** (`px148-run.mjs`, the same steps in both engines):
   1. release any hold;
   2. position the page (rest, or the 10-frame swipe step);
   3. hold on (Web Animations and SMIL paused, page timers and frames held);
   4. settle: shots 250 ms apart until two in a row are equal outside the masks, at most 8 shots. The last one is the base;
   5. arm style on, shot;
   6. arm style off, shot (base-again);
   7. hold off.
   - Geometry is read at all three shots.
   - Held-callback counts are kept per phase (settle, arm, back).
   - Every shot of a triple that is not T0, not settled, or shows any A/A pixel is saved: base, arm and base-again, in
     both engines (`out/deck-contain/px148-shots/`, `px148-sim-shots/`).
4. **Null arm N0**: an empty `<style>` element, toggled exactly like an arm. Every position and mode gets an N0 triple
   and an R3 triple; the order alternates by position (`armOrder`: top and inview N0 first, enter and leave R3 first).
5. **No retries.** P-146 shot a T1 pair twice; P-148 takes one triple per key.

## Masks and R3's footprint

Masks:
- the hero clock (+4 CSS px, as in P-143 and P-146);
- in the Simulator, the screen outside the page viewport, from the magenta-marker calibration (status bar, toolbar,
  landscape insets: no page pixel).

Nothing else is masked: M1, M2 and M4-M8 are frozen, not masked.

**The footprint F** is the deck's border box plus 24 CSS px, clipped to the viewport, read at every triple. A mask that
meets F is dropped for that triple and recorded (`maskDropped`).

**Why nothing outside F can change under R3:**
1. **CSS scope.** R3 is one rule, `.start-selling-profile-deck` inside the mobile media query. It sets `height`,
   `padding-block` and `align-items`, none of which is inherited. It can change only the deck's own box and the layout
   of the deck's children.
2. **The deck's box size is unchanged.** In P-146's pixel records, R3's deck height and document height equal the base's
   in all 14 Chrome configs and all 3 Simulator states, to 0.001 px (`deckH`, `docH` in `out/deck-contain/px-*.json`).
   So no box outside the deck moves.
3. **Children are clipped.** At mobile widths the deck is a scroller (`overflow: auto/hidden`, GEO in
   `geo-chrome2.json` and `geo-sim3.json`). Its children paint only inside its padding box, and R3 changes no deck
   shadow or outline.
4. **P-146's R3 differences outside the deck.** The reader checked Chrome block by block: in 40 of 41 Chrome FAILs,
   every pixel above 8 levels sits in a block that also moves base vs base-again. The one exception is t1024x1366 top
   swipe, at shade 24, which lies outside the deck's band. These differences are instrument motion, not a shown R3
   change. This is OBSERVED, and this run tests it again in point 5.
5. **Checked again in this run.** The scorer counts every R3 pixel more than 8 levels beyond A/A that lies outside F
   (`beyondOutsideF`; prediction P9). Those pixels are not masked, so they still count toward R3's tier.
6. **Geometry is checked per triple.** R3's geometry (deck box, every shell's size and offset, section, next section,
   document height, scroll position) must equal the base's at every triple, or the triple is T3.

## Rules (frozen; scorer `score-p148.mjs`, self-test `selftest-p148.mjs`)

A pixel's level is its largest per-channel difference.
- **A/A** is base vs base-again.
- **Beyond A/A** (T2 counting): level > 8 where the A/A level is <= 8.
- **Fill or edge:** a pixel is fill when its 3x3 neighbourhood in the base varies by <= 2 levels per channel
  (`research/pixel-tolerance/README.md` §1); otherwise it is an edge.

**Step 1, the instrument gate, per engine.** It is computed over every registered key: 224 Chrome triples (14 configs
x 4 positions x 2 modes x N0 and R3) and 48 Simulator triples.

| check | Chrome | Simulator |
|---|---|---|
| G1 complete: every registered triple present, from complete files | yes | yes |
| G2 settled: every triple's base settled (two equal shots within 8) | yes | yes |
| G3 no A/A pixel above 8 levels (outside the masks), in every triple, N0 and R3 | 0 px | 0 px |
| G4 A/A pixels at any level, as a share of the frame, in every triple | <= 0.01 % | <= 0.01 % |
| G5 null arm: every N0 triple T0 or T1, with no geometry change | yes | yes |
| G6 the page did not move between base and base-again (geometry, scroll position) | yes | yes |

Why these thresholds:
- G3 = 0, because T2 counts only pixels more than 8 levels beyond A/A. Any A/A pixel above 8 is a blind spot for T2's
  share and area.
- G4 = 0.01 %, so the region where T1 could absorb an R3 difference is at most 1/50 of T2's 0.5 % share limit.
- The same numbers hold in both engines; nothing in P-146 justified a looser Simulator number.

**The gate passes** in an engine when G1-G6 all hold.
- **Either engine fails: the card ends.** The verdict is `GATE-FAILED <engine>`, with the failing checks and their
  triples. The scorer computes and prints nothing about R3.

**Step 2, R3** (only when both gates pass). Per R3 triple, the tier:
- **T0**: no arm pixel differs.
- **T1**: every arm pixel that differs also differs in A/A.
- **T2**: all of the following:
  - fill pixels beyond A/A <= 64 levels;
  - edge pixels <= 128;
  - largest shade (any arm pixel where A/A is 0) <= 128;
  - beyond-A/A pixels <= 0.5 % of the frame;
  - <= 2,000 CSS px.
  - The area is the triple's total, which is stricter than T2's "per element".
- **T3**: anything else.
- Any R3 geometry change in the triple makes it T3 (T2 (c): nothing moved).

Per engine the worst tier is reported, with the five numbers of the worst triple: largest shade, share beyond A/A, area in
CSS px, edge or fill, static (rest) or moving (swipe end, frozen).

The overall verdict is `R3 T0`, `R3 T1`, `R3 T2` or `R3 T3`, with these rules:
- **Reduced motion exact** (T2 (d)): an R3 triple at m430x775-rm above T1 makes the verdict T3.
- **Motion-only** (memory reading): a swipe triple at T2 or T3 whose rest triple (same config and position) is T0 or T1
  makes the verdict T3.
- **HELD-EXTRA:** R3 held more than 2 page frames or timeouts in its arm phase than in its back phase (intervals
  excluded). The arm shot may then lack an effect the page applies through a held callback, so the verdict is
  `R3 UNDECIDED-HELD`, unless it is already T3.
- **Reported, no rule:**
  - R3 beyond-A/A pixels outside F (P9);
  - dropped masks (P10);
  - R3 at d1440x900, where R3's media query does not match (P8);
  - per-phase held counts;
  - the settle shot counts.
- **T2 (c)** (only anti-aliased edges or sub-pixel phase; nothing recoloured or re-worded) cannot be computed. The result
  reader reads it on the saved shots of every T2 triple.
- **T2 (g)** (a moving part within 1 device px at every frame) is partly covered: the frozen swipe-end triples, plus
  P-146's P16 (R3's switch point equals the base in both engines). Real motion stays with the P14 phone G check and the
  user.

## Known limits (disclosed now)

- **The hold covers only callbacks registered through the three window functions.** A callback that R3 itself wakes and
  that runs through a held timer or frame is not in the arm shot. HELD-EXTRA detects the common case; an effect applied
  directly by an observer callback is not held and does show.
- A `cancelAnimationFrame` on a frame the hold has re-queued does not cancel it (a page effect after the hold ends; it does
  not touch the shots).
- **Simulator swipe mode.** The settle step waits for the scroll indicator to fade, so "swipe" there means "swipe end,
  settled".
- **The Simulator has no reduced-motion state** in P-146's set, and P-148 keeps that set. Reduced motion is judged in
  Chrome only.
