Every agent pulls its own next step, sends each result straight to the agents that use it, and prepares the next
step while it waits. Nobody waits for the lead or the user to order routine work. Rules R1-R8: `fixes/AGENT_ROLES.md`
"Proactive by default".

**Why:** user T-160 #7 (2026-10-04 21:18Z): "our team should not have reactive agents, just waiting me to order … work
together without any conflict". On that day the lead made 89 of 92 queue moves by hand, the phone idled twice with no
job prepared, and the lead relayed results between agents. One user message got two different answers.
Evidence: `research/management/roles-v3-proactive.md`.

**How to apply:** at the end of a step, take the next step in your topic. Park only when no next step is left. Put
`decision:lead` only on a real lead decision (sign, land, rank, spawn, rule, user). Send results to the integrator,
mgmt-ops, mgmt-devices and the owners of affected topics, plus one FYI line to the lead. A phone ticket goes to ops
only when complete. In a shared thread, only the question's owner answers. Related: [[lead-owns-issue-loop]],
[[lead-delegates-areas-measures]], [[busy-workers-answer-later]].
