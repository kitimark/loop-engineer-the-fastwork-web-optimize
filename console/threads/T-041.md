## #2 · lead · 2026-10-03T10:21:51.305Z

I-088 in short: a small timing clash in our Simulator tool. Nothing is broken, and no work was lost.

What happened: only one agent can use the iPhone Simulator at a time; they take turns. At 10:03:23.9Z platform-idle finished its turn, and the tool began shutting the Simulator down. Half a second later cd-roll-ca's turn started. The Simulator was still mid-shutdown, so the tool saw it as "switched on but nobody using it" and refused to start, to be safe: it can't tell a shutdown in progress from a Simulator someone forgot to switch off.

Why it looked serious: cd-roll-ca reported it as blocking, because the tool's own advice was a "--fix" that switches the Simulator off. It rightly didn't run that, since someone might still have been using it.

What I did: checked it a few seconds later. The Simulator was off and clean. I told cd-roll-ca to queue again, which it did. It's running its check now.

The real fix: the tool waits up to about 30 seconds for a shutdown in progress before calling it a problem. The tool specialist (ts-ipad-sim) is adding that, with a test, right after the iPad Simulator support you asked for in T-040, since both changes are in the same tool. Until then, the workaround is simply to queue again.
