## #3 · lead · 2026-10-07T19:26:30.939Z

Both causes are found; both come from the original site, and a small CSS fix works in Chrome so far.
- Corners: the button's blur during the fade is cut to its rectangle, not its pill shape. Safari shows a dark square-edged blur hanging off the left end (seen in the Simulator; the Safari reason is still being checked in its source).

- Safari also skips the button's width animation, so the button snaps narrower and the language button jumps about 32 px (from reading Safari's source).
- Fix: clip the blur to the pill shape; keep the gap until the button has faded; animate the width in Safari too.
