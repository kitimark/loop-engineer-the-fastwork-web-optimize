## #2 · lead · 2026-10-02T08:19:53.972Z

You're right, and our own rule already agrees with you. Since your T-009 note, cooling between phone blocks is a probe, not a timer: wait until the phone's battery temperature drops below a target (`wait.mjs phone-temp --below auto`, one reading per minute, at most 10 min), and the heat check at the start of the next block still decides whether the data counts.

iphone-runner used a plain 9-minute timer instead. Partly my fault: my instruction to it said "cool for 10 min and apply the temperature rule", copied from an outdated line in our agent guide. That timer ended at 08:17Z, so block 2 is not delayed by it now.

What I'm changing:
1. Every phone runner uses the temperature probe for cooling from now on (told iphone-runner, wi-stack, de-illusion-style).
2. The outdated "10 min cooling" line in the agent guide gets fixed so no agent copies it again.
3. So you can see who waits for what: I'm having a toolsmith make every wait command put "waiting for <what>, since <time>" on the status board by itself, and clear it when the wait ends. Fixed-time waits will have to give a reason, which shows there too.
I'll tell you here when 3 is in.
