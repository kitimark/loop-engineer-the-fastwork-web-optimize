## #1 · lead · 2026-10-03T19:30:34.180Z

What we found (de-breakpoints, Chrome, 1440 px wide, Reduce Motion on):
- The original page shows the 10 hero photo cards in one of two ways. On a slow first load they are sharp. On a quick reload they come out slightly soft (about 3 % of the screen, mostly photo texture).
- Our scroll-engine fix (st6-rb2, already in the best build) almost always shows the sharp version.
- Why: both builds decode the photos identically (checked against Chrome's source). The soft version is a first drawing that the original page never redraws. Our fix happens to redraw it once, so it ends up sharp, just like the original after any redraw. An invisible redraw on the original made it sharp too, 2 of 2 tries. Why Chrome's first drawing is soft is still unknown (observed in our setup only).
- With Reduce Motion off there is no difference at all: 2 px on one card.

My reading of your pixel rule (PR-010): the original's own range of pictures includes the sharp one, so the fix stack passes (within the original's own noise). It does not draw a new picture. Strictly comparing quick reload against quick reload it would be a 3 % difference, so I'm telling you rather than deciding silently.

Nothing for you to do unless you disagree. If you do, reply here and I'll turn it into a proposal.
