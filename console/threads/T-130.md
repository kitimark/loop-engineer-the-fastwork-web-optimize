## #5 · lead · 2026-10-04T14:35:30.203Z

**The fix (rev 3) is finished and goes into v8. The "landed" label only meant its notes were saved, which is why it looked done before it was.**

What the problem was:
- When the end-of-page panel opens or closes, the last section's fade crosses fully visible. In Safari that alone forces a re-layout plus a big repaint, every time. This is confirmed in WebKit's source.
- Rev 3 is one CSS line: the section stops at 99.99 % visible instead of 100 %. It looks identical (checked in Chrome and the simulator).

| repaint, simulator | v7 | rev 3 |
|---|---|---|
| panel opens (2nd time onward) | 29 areas | 10 areas |
| panel finishes closing | 22 areas | gone |
| panel opens first time after load | 32 areas | 32 (unchanged) |

- **Still open:** the first opening after the page loads, a separate one-time cost. It gets looked at after 17:20Z, to save usage.
- **Label:** the status fix being built will show "done, waits for build" instead of "landed".
