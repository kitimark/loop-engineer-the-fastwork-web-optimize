## #2 · lead · 2026-10-01T21:31:47.039Z

The phone was too hot (41.5 °C) when the test started. Our first check run before each test failed: the page scrolled at about half its normal smoothness. A hot iPhone slows itself down, so any numbers from it would be wrong. Our rule then said: cool a fixed 10 minutes and restart.
The fixed 10 minutes is a safe guess, not a measurement, so I've changed it. The phone's temperature is now read every minute, and the test restarts as soon as it is back at the temperature where the check passed before (about 38 °C). The check run still decides whether the results count.
Things you can do that help it cool faster (only if convenient):
- take the case off;
- keep it off a warm surface and out of the sun;
- lower the screen brightness.
Charging adds heat too: we're checking whether the cable to the Mac is charging it during tests.

## #4 · user · 2026-10-01T21:33:24.078Z

The iphone can check the temp ?

## #5 · lead · 2026-10-01T21:33:35.401Z

Yes. The Mac reads the phone's battery temperature over the USB cable, through Apple's diagnostics interface (the same one that reports charge level and charging state). Our tool logs it at the start and end of every test run. That's where the 41.5 °C and 38.7 °C come from.
One limit: it is the battery's sensor, not the chip's. The chip runs hotter and changes faster, so the battery reading trails it. That's why the check run before each test, not the temperature, still decides whether a test counts. The temperature only tells us when to try again.

## #13 · lead · 2026-10-01T22:13:36.677Z

Cooldown report from iphone-runner (our own readings, few samples, so treat as rough):
- Parked on a blank page, the phone cooled from 41.1 to 36.7 °C in 11 minutes. That's about 0.6 °C/min near 40 and 0.3 °C/min near 38.
- Time to get down to 38 °C: about 4 min after a typical block (39.8 °C), about 5.5 min from 41 °C.
- Left on the animated page it barely cooled (0.1 °C/min) or even warmed, 39.7 → 41.0 °C in 22 min. Parking cools 3-5x faster. That was the real reason the cooling took so long.
- Heat only seemed to hurt one kind of run, the morph test: it failed once at 41.5 °C, while every other check passed from 35.6 up to 42.5 °C.
New rule: park Safari after every block, then wait for the temperature (expect 4-6 min instead of a fixed 10), and start morph blocks at 39.5 °C or below. One correction: the phone was charging for a while today (01:38-01:56 your time); my earlier 'never charging' was wrong for that stretch.
