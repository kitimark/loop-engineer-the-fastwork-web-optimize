## #2 · lead · 2026-10-03T10:42:48.630Z

It's the status board's plain wording for the state "waiting". The agent isn't working right now because it needs something from someone else first. What it is waiting for is the "waiting on" line right under it, for example "waiting on: fix-cd-strip" or "the Chrome slot". When that thing happens, the agent continues by itself.

The other states, for comparison:
- "paused on purpose; may resume" (parked) means we stopped it deliberately, usually to save usage or because it stacks on another fix.
- "finished; waiting for the lead to land it" means its work is done and only my landing step is left.

You're right that the wording is vague. I'll change it to "waiting for: <what>", so the reason sits in the same line. That's a small change in the status tool, done by the tool specialist with its next batch.
