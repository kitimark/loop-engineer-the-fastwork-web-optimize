## #9 · fix-hero-exit-threaded · 2026-10-04T13:17:53.322Z

(People word with the avatars) The avatar "O" is cheap by itself. It stutters because only the main processor can animate it, and that processor is busy on every frame at this spot.

- Where: the O in "People". 1.3 s after the title comes into view, the avatars stack in and the O widens over about 0.9 s. When you scroll away it shrinks, and coming back replays it.
- Why it can't use the GPU: the O widens by changing its layout width (its columns, its height, and each avatar's width and margins). Browsers run size changes on the main processor only, never on the GPU (CONFIRMED in Chrome and WebKit source; see research/gpu-props).
- Why the main processor is busy: at that spot the iOS Simulator re-lays out the whole screen about once per frame, and the O is not the cause (counts only, OBSERVED):

| 2 s at the People title (Simulator) | full-screen layouts |
|---|---|
| as it is now | 56-73 |
| O's animation off | 70 |
| all page animations paused | 5 |

- What causes those layouts: animations from the hero, which is still on screen above the title (the accent word's stroke, the button glow and others). Which one is not pinned down yet. On the phone a layout at this spot takes about 13 ms (fix-toolbar-resize's data), against 17 ms per frame.
- Images: the 6 avatar photos are 480-730 px wide but are shown at 108 px (about 300 KB in total). Smaller files would download faster and use less memory, but would not make the motion smoother: they are decoded once (OBSERVED in Chrome).
- Phone: we have no recording of the O opening yet. The robot and finger runs pass the title in 0.1-0.3 s, too fast for it to open.

Proposal idea, needs your OK and a Try-it: widen the O with GPU moves (the avatars slide and scale inside a clip) instead of changing its width, and serve right-sized avatar images. The start and end look the same; the in-between frames differ slightly.
