## #94 · lead · 2026-10-05T04:04:43.414Z

No, a GIF can't play at 60 fps. 50 fps is the fastest that plays smoothly.
- A GIF stores each frame's time in steps of 10 ms (GIF89a spec), so it can say 20 ms (50 fps) or 10 ms (100 fps), never 16.7 ms (60 fps).
- The only way to average 60 fps is the pattern 20, 20, 10 ms. But browsers show any frame of 10 ms or less for 100 ms.
- Chrome source (deferred_image_decoder.cc, "durations <= 10 ms ... use 100 ms") CONFIRMED; Safari has the same rule from WebKit bug 36082 (not checked in its current source).
- So a "60 fps" GIF would stall every third frame: much worse than 50 fps.
| GIF frame time | fps | plays as |
|---|---|---|
| 20 ms | 50 | smooth (our plan) |
| 20, 20, 10 ms | 60 avg | stalls 100 ms every 3rd frame |
| 10 ms | 100 | about 10 fps |
- True 60 fps needs video: the MP4 here in the article, or a YouTube/Vimeo embed on Medium.
