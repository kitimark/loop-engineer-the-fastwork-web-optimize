## #6 · lead · 2026-10-02T09:38:59.581Z (asks)

The design for retriable steps (I-059). What you would see:

1. Parts that end by themselves have no Done button. Before you start one, it says "After Start, don't tap anything: this part ends by itself". While it runs, you see "Running, please wait", the runner's latest note and a timer. No reminders nag you meanwhile. This is what went wrong in C-021.
2. "Made a mistake? Redo this part" on the running part, and "Redo part N" on finished parts. Both ask once to confirm. That part and the ones after it start again. If the phone needs resetting first, the page says "Part N again (try 2). Wait: the runner is resetting the phone", and the runner is told at once.
3. "Start over" for the whole step, also with a confirm.
4. Undo for an accidental Done: after you tap Done, the page shows "Done. Saving in 10 s... [Undo]". Your time isn't held up; only the runner hears about it 10 s later.
5. Every attempt is kept separately (try 1, try 2, ...), so a recording is always matched to the right attempt.

One choice for you:
- (a) the 10-second undo on every Done (my suggestion);
- (b) no undo window unless a step asks for it.
