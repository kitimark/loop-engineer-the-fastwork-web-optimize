## #2 · lead · 2026-10-03T10:37:35.951Z

Good idea. Here's how I'd build it:

- On every console page (Your steps, Proposals, Threads, Status, Progress), you can point at any item, or select some text, and press "Comment".
- A small box opens next to it. You type your question and send.
- The comment becomes a thread that remembers exactly where it came from: the page, the item (e.g. "PR-009, option both, picture 2" or "status row cd-strip"), and the text you selected. The thread shows that as a quote at the top, and the item gets a small comment marker so you can find the conversation again.
- Who gets it: the answer box shows "Your comment goes to: lead" (same labels as T-038). I read it and answer, or hand it to the agent that owns that item, and you see who it went to.
- Everything stays on this Mac, like the rest of the console.

Timing: the same tool specialist who is adding images to threads (ts-thread-images) builds this right after, because both changes touch the threads code. Doing them one after the other avoids conflicts. I'll post here when it's live.

## #10 · ts-proposal-talk · 2026-10-03T15:56:39.171Z

What was wrong: only some things on each page could take a comment: the rows of the Status table, the fix cards on Progress, step cards, the parts of an open proposal, and the messages inside a thread. Everything else had no comment spot, for example the proposal list, the thread list, the Waiting on you panel, headings and filters, and the Right now and timeline parts of Progress. Most of what you see first on a page was in that group, so nothing happened there.

The fix: every line you can see will take a comment. A line that belongs to something (a workstream, a step, a proposal, a thread) is a comment on that thing. Your example in Waiting on you becomes a comment on that workstream (Countdown roll as one GPU animation per digit), with the line you pointed at quoted. Anything else becomes a comment on the page, also with the line you pointed at quoted. You hover or click a line, or select some text, then press Comment, as before.

When: I'm writing the tests now. It goes live once the lead lands it and restarts the console, today. I'll post here when it's ready.
