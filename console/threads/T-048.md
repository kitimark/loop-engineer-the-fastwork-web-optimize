## #3 · ts-proposal-talk · 2026-10-03T15:31:53.445Z

Design for talking inside a proposal (plain words):

What works today: on a proposal you can click Comment (or select some text) on the whole card, an option or a screenshot. That starts a thread for the lead, and the item shows a small "1 comment" marker that links to the Threads page. The answer only shows on the Threads page.

What's new:
1. A "Questions and answers" box on each proposal page, above "Your decision". It shows every question and comment on that proposal, with the answers (pictures too) right there. Each one has its own reply box, so you can keep asking in place.
2. "Ask a question" at the top of that box, with "About:" (the whole proposal, the option on screen, or a part such as "What it saves", "Risk", "What looks different"). The Comment button works on those parts now too.
3. Your question goes to the agent who wrote the proposal, and to the lead. The page says who. The author answers, or the lead when nobody owns it any more.
4. A question is never a decision. Nothing changes until you press Accept, Reject or Request change. If an answer says "I'll fix that in the next revision", that only happens after you request the change.

For agents: questions are ordinary console threads, so `thread wait` wakes the author and the lead, and they answer with `thread reply`. New: `proposal talk <id>` lists the questions on one proposal. I'm building it now; reply here if you want it different.

It's built and all tests pass. It goes live as soon as the lead adds it and restarts the console, and I'll post here when that's done.

What you'll see on a proposal:
- Under each proposal, a "Questions and answers" box. Every question on that proposal is a small thread there, with the agent's answers and a reply box, like this page.
- "Ask a question" at the top of the box. You can also press Comment on a part of the proposal, such as "What it saves" or "Risk", and the question names that part.
- The box says who reads your question: the agent who wrote the proposal, and the lead.
- In the proposals list, a proposal with questions shows a small count.
- Asking is never a decision. Nothing changes until you press Accept, Reject or Request change.
