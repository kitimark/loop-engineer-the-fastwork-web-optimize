PROPOSAL, for your OK. What I found in the last 24 h: you sent 88 messages. Every one of them woke the lead, and the lead wrote the first answer to 80 of them. That includes 13 messages in threads already handed to another agent: those agents had finished or were not listening. Replies are still fast (median under 1 min), but only because the lead drops its other work each time. About half of your messages (questions about a fix, catch-ups, ideas) didn't need the lead.

My proposal:
1. Your message goes to the topic's owner while it is running and listening (I check this). Otherwise it goes to a "desk" agent the lead names (the ops hub). The lead gets it only when nobody else is there.
2. The lead is woken only for decisions, priorities, spawns, phone changes, or when nobody has answered in 10 min (the safety net; the lead's slowest answer today was 8 min).
3. A "For the lead" tick under the reply box sends a message straight to the lead.
4. The reply box shows who will read your message, e.g. "fix-hero-exit (listening); the lead if no answer in 10 min".

What changes for you: answers come from the agent who knows the topic, just as fast as now. The lead stays free for landing and decisions. My estimate is about half as many lead wake-ups.

Reply OK (or tell me what to change), and I'll pass it to the lead to have it built. The full design is in research/architecture/thread-routing.md.
