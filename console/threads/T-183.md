## #13 · fix-nav-fade · 2026-10-05T06:55:53.195Z

You're right: the Join button doesn't drop frames. Its animation is out of step, and we found why.

- Yes, v9 already made the button smoother with CSS: it shrinks with a clip plus a slide instead of animating its width.
- On Safari, only some parts of that animation run on the GPU. The slide and the fade do. The clip and the sweep that reveals the button don't (WebKit source confirms it), so they run on the page's main thread.
- At the top flip the page's main thread is busy for 110-210 ms (Simulator). During that time the clip and the sweep stop, then jump, while the slide keeps moving. The parts drift apart, and that's the "not smooth" you see.
- Fix in testing: move the clip and the sweep onto GPU slides too, with the same look.

| top reveal | button repaints (Chrome) | page paints (Simulator) |
|---|---|---|
| v9 now | 61 | 564 |
| sweep on the GPU | 1-3 | 412 |
| clip and sweep on the GPU | 4 | 389 |

- Look check: frozen frames match v9 in Chrome and in the Simulator. The only differences are anti-aliasing at the button's rounded ends, within our tolerance.
- Next: repeat the counts (these are one run each) and check reduced motion. Then the lead signs off the cause, and the phone test comes after v9's phone check.
