## #2 · lead · 2026-10-02T22:09:53.891Z

Yes, it can be done. Here is what I found and what I'll build.

Why it doesn't work today: our mirror server sends "X-Frame-Options: SAMEORIGIN" (the same header fastwork.com sends). That header lets a page be framed only by a page from the same origin, and the origin includes the port. The console runs on port 5559 and the mirror servers run on other ports, so the browser refuses to show them inside a proposal. (Spec reference: HTML Standard, "X-Frame-Options" and "same origin" = scheme + host + port.)

Options:
A) On the Mac (recommended, building now): add a "Try it" panel to the proposal page with two phone-sized frames side by side, 430 x 932, original on the left and PoC on the right, each scrolled on its own with the trackpad. The console starts the two mirror servers itself when you open the panel and stops them when you close it. Only for these compare servers, the mirror server gets an opt-in switch that allows framing by the console page and nothing else. Normal serving and all measurements keep the current header.
   Limits:
   - This is desktop Chrome, not iPhone Safari.
   - Scrolling is trackpad/mouse, not touch.
   - Both pages run in one tab and share the Mac. Good for comparing how it looks (the countdown rolls, the menu morph, the carousel), not for judging speed. For "feel" there is also a one-at-a-time toggle that loads only the frame you are looking at.
B) On the iPhone: the honest test stays the blind swipe step you already know: each page full screen in Safari, A then B. Putting both into a proposal page on the phone would need the console to be reachable over Wi-Fi (today it is localhost only, on purpose). An iframe on the phone also loses the toolbar collapse and the real page scroll. So I won't open the console to Wi-Fi unless you ask for it.
C) Side-by-side videos of the phone screen (the safe recorder from T-019) can go into a proposal as well, if you want to see the phone without holding it.

Plan: a toolsmith builds A with tests (about 1-2 h). I'll tell you here when PR-007's countdown has a working "Try it" panel. The panel also shows a note while a measurement is running, so your scrolling doesn't disturb it.

## #4 · lead · 2026-10-02T22:11:47.495Z

Yes, that is exactly the change being built (incident I-074). We don't remove the header everywhere, only on the two servers the proposal panel starts: on those, X-Frame-Options goes away and a 'frame-ancestors' rule allows the console page (localhost:5559) and nothing else. Every other server, including the ones that take measurements, keeps the header unchanged. That way earlier and later measurements still compare (the headers are part of what the page gets), and no other site can frame the mirror. A test checks that the default headers stay byte-identical. I'll write here when the panel works on PR-007.
