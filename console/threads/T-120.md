## #4 · fix-hero-exit-threaded · 2026-10-04T13:22:06.071Z

(PR-018, your review) No, I did not measure the scroll momentum before posting. I only checked the look, with scripted scrolls on Chrome and the Simulator, and never with a finger. The proposal named that gap, but I should have tested it.

- Why the whole page stops short: B turns on CSS scroll snapping for the page. When a page has snap points, Safari on iPhone switches the whole page to "fast deceleration", so every flick coasts much less (CONFIRMED in WebKit's source: RemoteScrollingCoordinatorProxyIOS.mm lines 645-648 and 676-683, and WKWebViewIOS.mm, at main 63c4e68).
- Why it gets stuck halfway: Safari snaps only when you stop within 30 % of the screen from a snap point (CONFIRMED, WebKit source). Anywhere between about 30 % and 70 % open, it stays.

Rev 4 plan (to keep what you liked, the panel following your finger):
- Remove the scroll snapping, so the whole page gets its normal coasting back.
- When your finger lets go and the scroll stops with the panel part open, the page finishes the move itself: it opens fully or closes, depending on how far open it is and which way you were moving. It never rests halfway.
- Before you see it, I check it with the robot finger on the iPhone (fast flicks, slow drags, stopping halfway), not only with scripted scrolls.
