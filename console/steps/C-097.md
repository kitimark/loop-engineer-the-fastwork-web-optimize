## Instructions

The iPhone still reports Critical at 35 C, so Xcode's condition may not be on Serious. On the Mac, in Xcode choose Window > Devices and Simulators, select the iPhone, and look at Device Conditions > Thermal State. In the notes, write what it shows. Then click Stop, wait about 2 minutes, choose Thermal State 'Serious' and click Start. Finally unlock the iPhone (enter the passcode if asked), check Settings > Developer > Enable UI Automation is on, go to the Home Screen and press Done here.
