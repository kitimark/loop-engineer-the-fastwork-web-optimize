## Instructions

On the Mac, in Xcode choose Window > Devices and Simulators, click Devices and select the iPhone; under Device Conditions set Condition to Thermal State and Profile to Serious, then click Start. Leave it on (keep the cable in: it ends when the phone disconnects from Xcode). The serious profile wants the phone at Serious induced by Xcode. Then click Done here; art-worst-cases runs again.
