## Problem

Your note on rev 2: "I think your measure might varies from the another stuff of content and components in the fastwork source code. it might impact some how.

do you measure only your part without any components ?"

Short answer: no. Every phone number in rev 1 and rev 2 was measured on the full Fastwork page, with all its other parts loaded and running. You are right: they affect the numbers.

Where to read what:
- How the 5 ways were measured: the box "Idea".
- What the rest of the page does to the numbers: the box "Looks same".
- What the numbers can and cannot say, and one correction to rev 2: the box "Risk".
- Ways to measure the word alone: the options at the bottom, each with its phone time. Nothing changes on the page until you pick one.

What this means, in short:
1. The comparison between the ways is fair: each ran on the same page, in the same block, with the same other parts. Only the word changed.
2. But the hitch counts are not the word's own. On today's SVG, the 2 s windows around 'you' cover 18 % of the time and hold 13 % of the page's frames over 50 ms. Most hitches happen elsewhere on the page (OBSERVED).
3. So the small differences between the smooth ways (1-3 frames per run) may come from the rest of the page. Only the freezes (tiles, boxes) are clearly the word's own.
4. To get the word's own cost it has to be measured alone. I recommend the option "Word alone".

## Idea

HOW THE 5 WAYS WERE MEASURED

Phone timing (iPhone 15 Pro Max, Safari): the full page.
- Our local copy of the real homepage, every part loaded and running.
- Each run left the page at the top for 44 s, untouched.
- In view at the top: the hero title with the accent word, and the hero's other moving parts. The countdown and the photo cards change on their own (OBSERVED in Chrome: they differ between two loads).
- Parts lower on the page are off screen, but their code is loaded too. Whether all of it stops off screen was not checked (UNRESOLVED).
- The accent word cycles through 7 words in different languages. Only 'you' was changed; the other 6 drew with today's SVG in every run, every way.
- Hitches were counted in a 2 s window from each 'you' draw start, 4 windows per run. A hitch in that window can come from any part of the page.

Timeline counts (the browser's own work log, phone): the full page too. Each count is the whole page's work during that draw, not the word's alone.

Chrome counts (earlier, in PR-001): the full page.

Simulator: looks only, as frame-by-frame pixel checks with the clock stopped. Once, a page with only the word and no Fastwork code was used, but only to see whether the canvas stalls there too, not to compare the ways.

So: nothing in rev 1 or rev 2 measured the word alone.

## Looks the same?

WHAT THE REST OF THE PAGE DOES TO THE NUMBERS

One work step per frame, for the whole page. Before each screen update the browser runs the animations, then the frame callbacks, then style and layout for the whole document in one go (CONFIRMED: HTML standard, "update the rendering" steps, html.spec.whatwg.org/multipage/webappapis.html: "Recalculate styles and update layout for doc"). So the word and the other parts share each frame. If that frame is late, it is late for all of them.

The rest of the page works in the same frames. With the word on a picture or a video (no style work of its own), the page still did 30-43 style recalcs and 4-6 layouts per 0.9 s draw (OBSERVED, Timeline, blocks A and B). That is mostly the rest of the page. With today's SVG it is 57-60 and 56-58, about one per frame. The browser counts per frame, not per part, so these counts cannot be split cleanly into "word" and "rest".

Where the hitches are. Today's SVG, 5 runs (OBSERVED):
- The 'you' windows: 18 % of the time, 4 of the 31 frames over 50 ms (13 %), 28 of the 226 over 34 ms (12 %).
- The rest of the time, including the other 6 words, holds the other 87-88 %.

Noise from run to run. The same SVG build had 0 to 12 frames over 50 ms in the whole 44 s (OBSERVED). That noise sits under every comparison.

Heat. The rest of the page keeps the phone working the whole time. The check runs before and after each block passed, so heat did not change the result (OBSERVED). How much heat the rest adds was not measured.

Compositing (putting the page's layers together into the screen picture). The word is part of the same screen picture as the rest; how much they share there is UNRESOLVED.

## Risk

WHAT THE NUMBERS CAN AND CANNOT SAY
- They can say which way is worse or equal on the real page, which is what a visitor gets. The freezes (tiles, boxes) are clear and are the word's own.
- They cannot say the word's own cost, or which smooth way is cheapest by itself. Those differences (1-3 frames per run) are within the rest of the page's noise.
- A word-alone test has its own limit: it shows the word's cost, not what you feel on the real page. The full-page numbers we have answer that half.

STILL NOT TESTED (as in rev 2): finger scrolling; long sessions; the other 6 words; the visual recording on the phone (deferred at your request).

CORRECTION TO REV 2. Rev 2 said block B's Timeline counts were not taken. They were (09:47-09:49Z); I had missed the files. Per 'you' draw: video 31-42 style recalcs, 4-6 layouts, 3-12 paints; visible copy 57-59, 57-59, 58-70, the same as the SVG. The verdict does not change.

TWO METHOD CHANGES (as in rev 2): the visual-defect rule changed after block A's runs, before analysis (7dbc973); the frame counter was fixed after the data (ec9b414).

Risk: It shows each way's own cost, not what you feel on the full page: a way that wins alone may still not win on the page. The full-page numbers we already have cover that. The video can be added with its start fixed, if you want it.
