## Problem

The end-screen backgrounds cause most of the slow frames after you lift the finger (phone test: 16 per run with them, 3 without).
- Your blind swipe agreed.
- The page downloads 89 picture files (10.4 MB) on a phone; 21 of them (3.3 MB) are never drawn there.
- 27 pictures are bigger than a phone screen can show; 10 photos are stored as PNG, a heavy format for photos.
- The end screen redraws grain mixed in with a blend effect, and a live-blurred photo, on every toolbar step.

## Options

### Option lighter-files: A: lighter files

Shrink 22 oversized pictures to exactly what a phone shows, same file type.
- 3 phone pixels per page pixel, so nothing gets blurry on the iPhone.
- The menu photo wall loads pictures only when shown: the 8 photos a phone hides are never downloaded.
- 5 more were left as they are: their shrunk file came out bigger than the original.

Risk: Lag effect not measured yet; the phone test after you pick decides.
- This test build swaps the files for every screen size; a real change would add phone sizes (srcset), so computers keep today's files.
- The hero intro could not be compared by screenshot: it differs between two loads of the same page.

Visual difference:
- A few avatars and seller photos, slightly softer at most; nothing moves.

### Option new-format: B: A plus AVIF

Everything in A, plus 32 pictures re-saved as AVIF.
- AVIF is a modern photo format Safari reads since iOS 16 (webkit.org, Safari 16.0 notes).
- The page links to the new files.

Risk: Same as A.
- AVIF takes a little more phone work to unpack the first time a picture appears; not measured on the phone yet.
- 3 seller photos whose links are built while the page runs keep their old format (shrunk only).

Visual difference:
- As A, plus faint compression changes in photos; nothing moves.

### Option baked-backgrounds: C: baked end-screen backgrounds

On phones the 4 end-screen background boxes show one still picture each, made from today's page.
- Grain with its blend effect, soft gradient and blurred photo are drawn once into the picture, not live on every redraw.
- Everything on top of them stays live; up to 640 px wide only.

Risk: Chrome cannot show the gain; the phone test after you pick decides.
- It may not remove the whole lag: the phone test also hid the animated colour titles at the end, and they redraw about 100 times per scroll. We are measuring that part separately now.
- Adds about 1 MB of download.
- On phones narrower than 430 px the grain is scaled slightly. Computers are unchanged.

Visual difference:
- The grain dots in the 4 end-screen boxes can sit in slightly different places; colours, sizes and positions are the same.

| what | before | after | tag | how |

| Live blend or blur layers at the end | 5 | 0 | OBSERVED | 2 stage grain blends, the invoice sheet's grain blend and mesh photo, 1 blurred stage photo. The agents panel's grain stays. |
| Picture download on a phone | 10.4 MB | 11.0 MB | OBSERVED | Chrome, one load and one full scroll. |
| Chrome paint events per scroll | 1306 | 1368 | OBSERVED | One run each; today's build varied 1306-1324 in 3 runs. Chrome blends on its GPU, so it cannot show this option's gain. |

## Recommended

Option baked-backgrounds: C is the only option aimed at the slow frames.
- It removes the live blend and blur work the phone test pointed at; A and B save download and memory, not redraw work.
- After you pick, a phone test measures the slow frames; A or B can be added on top.
