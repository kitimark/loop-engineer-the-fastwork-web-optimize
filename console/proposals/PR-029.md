Your notes, point by point:

1. Rev 1: "(b) has a bug: the background fades out sometimes at Serious."
- (b) is gone. (a) does not touch how the section fades: blur and transparency are exactly v14's.
- iPhone check at Serious: the fade, blur and transparency read the same in A and B at 4 positions during the fade.
- Smoothness: about the same. One or two more slower-than-34 ms frames in some runs. Frames over 50 ms: 3 per run in B, 2 in A, within what 4 runs can tell apart, and none longer (longest 55-56 ms in B, 57-58 ms in A).
- This is your look-and-feel call.

2. Rev 1: "try the arch-compositor-morph experiment instead." Its work is now part of v14, and (a) sits on top of it.

3. Rev 2: "Waiting for arch-compositor-morph done." Done: its menu-card work is in v14.

4. Rev 3: "Do later after the best build lands." v14 became the best build today (iPhone check P-161 passed), so rev 4 starts from it.

Background:
- At the end of the page the big end panel grows over the last section (the four feature rows).
- Over the first 55 % of that growth the section fades out: blurrier and more transparent, until it is invisible.
- v14 already pauses the section's 4 endless shimmers once it is fully invisible.
- (a) pauses them from the first moment of the fade instead. It is a one-line change.

## Idea

(a) freezes the section's shimmers from the first moment of the fade.
- They stop where they are when the fade starts.
- The blur and the fade stay exactly as in v14.
- Scroll back up and they continue from where they stopped.

In Try-it, A is v14 and B is v14 plus (a).

## Looks the same?

The blur, the fade, the panel and every colour are unchanged.
- Only the shimmers stop moving while the section fades.
- Most of that time the section is already blurred and half transparent.

## Risk

The gain is small: it saves animation work during the fade, not a visible stutter.
- The frozen shimmer is a visible change, small and only during the fade.

## Visual difference

- Still frames in Chrome: no difference, 0 px² at all 18 positions.
- Phone size (430x775), through the whole end morph, compared like for like.
- Your pixel rule (PR-010) allows at most 0.5 % of the screen.
- Still frames in the iPhone Simulator: at most 99 px² (0.025 % of the screen).
- All of it in the nav logo, which (a) never touches; it is also there where (a) does nothing: Simulator noise.
- The change is in motion only: the shimmers stop moving during the fade. A still-frame check can't see that, so please judge it in Try-it.
