## Problem

Your note on rev 2: the fade should not follow the finger; the top of the page feels smoother because a state starts it.
- Correction first: today's v11 already starts a timed fade when the menu passes 20 % open. Rev 2 said the button vanishes in one step; that was wrong (I compared resting shots, not frames).
- What still follows your finger in v11: the button's blur (0 to 10 px between 0 and 20 % open) and a 4 px nudge of the EN pill.
- The pill's shape change also runs on the page's main work loop on iPhone, not on the graphics side.

## Idea

B = v11 where nothing on the Join button follows your finger.
- Passing 20 % open starts one timed exit: fade and blur over 0.32 s after 0.12 s, the same transition the button uses to appear at the top of the page.
- EN slides over in 0.68 s on the same curve as at the top.
- Coming back: it returns only when the menu is fully closed, with the same timing in reverse.
- Graphics side only: see-through level, blur and position. The pill keeps its shape; no shape animation.
- The button sits on its own graphics layer the whole time, so starting a fade creates nothing new.
- Phones only (up to 640 px wide), like the rest of the end menu.

In Try-it, A is v11 (today's build) and B is rev 3.

## Looks the same?

At rest B looks the same as v11: menu closed, or 20 % or more open. Only the 0.44 s exit and the return differ, by design.

## Risk

It changes how the button leaves, so it needs your eye.
- The pill fades in place while EN slides over it; v11 narrows the pill as it fades.
- Small moves around 20 % can't make it blink: once hidden it stays hidden until the menu is fully closed (no change from v11).
- The timed button animation near the top of the page is unchanged.

## Visual difference

- During the exit: v11 narrows the dark pill while it fades; B fades it in place, EN slides over it.
- Between 0 and 20 % open: v11 blurs the button with your finger; B keeps it sharp until the timed exit starts.
- Closed, and 20 % or more open (after the exit): B is the same as v11.
