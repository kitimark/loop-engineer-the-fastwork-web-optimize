## Problem

Your report in T-245: when the end-of-page menu closes, the logo slides left and then jumps the last bit.
- Traced: in its last frame the logo jumps 15 pixels left in one step. It happens on phones only (screens up to 640 wide).
- Cause: on phones the logo is drawn at 75 % size. While the menu moves, it shrinks around its centre; at rest, around its left edge.
- When the menu has almost stopped, the page switches between the two in one frame, so the logo jumps.
- The original site does the same (also at the start of the opening: a 15-pixel jump to the right).
- v14 had fixed it; today's best build g0.0c brought back the original's end of the page, and this jump with it.

## Idea

One style line: the point the logo shrinks around moves smoothly from its left edge (menu closed) to its centre (menu open), together with the menu.
- The logo then takes one smooth path; the last frame moves it a tenth of a pixel instead of 15.
- Open menu, closed bar, tablet and desktop look exactly as today.
What you need to do: open A and B on the phone, scroll to the end, open and close the menu a few times, watch the logo top-left, and pick one.

## Looks the same?

Unchanged: the closed bar, the open menu, tablet and desktop widths, and reduced-motion mode.
- Only while the menu moves on a phone does the logo sit up to 15 pixels further left than today, so its path has no jump.
- Everything else on the screen matched at every checked moment of the open and the close (Chrome at phone sizes and the iOS Simulator).

## Risk

Low: one style line on the logo, only while the menu moves on phones.
- It is the rule v14 already had (from the Join button work, PR-021).
- No extra work per frame and no new layers: the logo already moves with the menu every frame.
- Stacks cleanly with the Join fix and the nav-box work in progress (both owners checked).

## Visual difference

- Phone, menu closing: the logo glides all the way to its place; the 15-pixel jump in the last frame is gone.
- Phone, menu opening: the logo starts its move smoothly instead of jumping 15 pixels right in the first frame.
- While the menu moves, the logo is up to 15 pixels left of where it is today (the same path the open/close takes without the jump).
