## Problem

On the iPhone the "people" word animation is over before its card fades in.
- The word: in "People still need people", the "o" turns into a row of faces and opens out (about 2 s).
- What starts the word today: the title being 35 % on screen. The card is still invisible then.
- The card's fade-in starts about 560 px further down (Chrome, phone size) and takes 0.9 s.
- At a gentle scroll (about 300 px a second) the word opened 0.65 s before the fade-in even started (Chrome check).
- The original site does the same; our fixes did not change it.

## Idea

Start the word when the card's fade-in has ended: the card fully shown, blur gone. The browser reports that moment itself, so there is no guessed timer.
- No extra wait after that: the 0.9 s fade-in is the time you see the text first.
- The title must be at least 35 % on screen, as before.
- Mirror for the reset: when the card starts to fade out (you scroll back up), the word closes again; the next fade-in plays it again.
Try it on the iPhone after the current phone tests; the lead tells you when.

## Looks the same?

The word's own motion is the same: same steps, same speed, same closing motion.
- Reduced motion: the same (the word shows open at once).
- Scrolling away past the title and back still replays the word, as before.
- Nothing else on the page changes; no style change.

## Options

### Option a: A: every width

At every width the word starts when the card's fade-in has ended, and closes when the card starts to fade out.
- On desktop it also waits for the fade-in now: at a brisk scroll about 0.2 s later than today.

Risk: Low: about 50 lines of script, no style change.
- A timing change you decide on: the word plays when the card is fully shown and closes when it fades out.
- The word's widening lays out its line for 0.9 s, as before; it now runs where you see it, right after the fade-in. Phone smoothness of that second is not measured yet (the phone is busy with tests).
- Desktop changes too: the word waits for the fade-in end and closes on the fade-out.

Visual difference:
- Phone and desktop: the "o" opens into faces right when the card is fully shown (0.9 s after the fade-in starts).
Before, it started 1.3 s after the title came in, often while the card was still invisible.
- Scrolling back up until the card fades out: the word closes (its own closing motion) and plays again on the next fade-in. Before, it stayed open.
- A quick scroll into the card and straight back out no longer plays the word unseen.
