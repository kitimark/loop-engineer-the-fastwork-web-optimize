## Problem

Rev 3, answering your notes on rev 2 (and thread T-158):
- "the animation lost during the end-of-page": yes, rev 1 and 2 made the button follow the panel. Rev 3 brings back the original's timed hide: the width closes over 0.68 s, the fade takes 0.32 s after 0.12 s.
- "the start of the page is not optimize but gpu yet ?": correct. In the original, the grow and shrink change the button's width and the gap, so the whole bar is laid out again every frame for 0.68 s.
- "i still see lag ... when it reveal and un-reveal at the start of the page": rev 3 keeps the same look and timing but draws it without that per-frame layout (numbers below).
- T-158 "could not depends on scroll velocity": agreed. Rev 3 does not follow the scroll at all. The bar flips at 72 % of the hero as in the original, and the button then runs on the original's 0.68 s timer.
- Extra (finding F-243, a code from our findings board): at the end of the close, the bar no longer restarts its own width animation about 5 times in the last 68 ms.

## Idea

Same look and timing as the original, drawn without moving the layout:
1. The button keeps its full width in the bar all the time. The dark pill inside it is cut to the original's width at each moment, and the language switch slides over by the same amount.
2. Each change (hidden to shown, or back) starts one short animation of that cut and slide on the original's 0.68 s curve. The fade and the text sweep are the original's own.
3. At the end of the page the button hides on the original's timer once the panel is 20 % open, and comes back the same way when it closes.
No script runs per frame while it plays.

## Looks the same?

Frame by frame it is the same picture as today's best build (v8).
- Top of the page, stills every 50-150 ms: at most 0.4 % of the bar's pixels differ, all on the edges of the moving language switch.
- At rest: identical.
- End of the page: the logo slides instead of jumping (rev 1, which you kept).

## Risk

Medium-low: about 90 lines of script and 70 of CSS on top of v8.
- The button gets an inner box for the dark pill (new markup); the click area is the same.
- Safari draws the cut of the pill on the main thread (paint only, no layout); the slide and the fade can run on the GPU (WebKit source, CONFIRMED). Safari numbers from the iOS Simulator follow in the comments.
- Reduced motion: no animation, as before.
- Not checked on the real iPhone; not timed (the numbers count layouts, not frame times).

## Visual difference

- Top of the page: nothing intended. The button grows and shrinks on the same timer and curve as today.
- End of the page: the original's timed hide is back (rev 1 and 2 had removed it). Rev 1's logo slide stays.
- While the language switch slides, the edges of its letters can differ by a fraction of a pixel.
