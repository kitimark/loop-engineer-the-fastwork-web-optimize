# PR-028: End-of-page menu panel: fit the visible iPhone screen when Safari's bottom bar shows (svh or dvh)

## Problem

On the iPhone the open end-of-page panel runs 28 px past the visible screen while Safari's bottom bar shows.
- Its rounded bottom corners and the Privacy Policy line sit under the bar.
- Cause: its height uses vh (1 % of the screen height), and Safari sizes vh as if its bar were hidden (WebKit source, confirmed).
- With the bar hidden it fits.

## Idea

Change one CSS unit in the panel height on phones (and in its copy used while the panel opens).
- svh: the screen height with the bar shown; it never changes.
- dvh: the screen height right now; it follows the bar.
- No script change.

## Looks the same?

Everything outside the panel stays the same.
- Inside, content is placed from the panel height: with a shorter panel the title, countdown and buttons sit about 20 px higher, the footer 40 px higher.

## Risk

Look change, so it is your choice.
- Measured in the iPhone Simulator and Chrome: counts and pixels, no timing.
- A short check on the real phone follows once it is free.

## Options

### Option dvh: dvh: always 12 px from the visible bottom

The panel's height follows the visible screen: 12 px gap at the bottom, like at the top, with the bar shown or hidden.

Risk: Extra work only while the bar slides and the panel opens or closes.
- Then the panel takes a new height each frame.
- Simulator, per such frame: no extra layout pass, about one more panel redraw (4 frames measured, small sample).

Visual difference:
- Bar shown: the panel ends 40 px higher (12 px gap); title, countdown and buttons ~20 px higher, footer 40 px higher
- Bar hidden: identical to today (0 pixels differ)
- Nothing outside the panel changes (Simulator, both bar states)

### Option svh: svh: always fits, fixed size

The panel is sized for the screen with Safari's bar shown. Its size never changes.

Risk: No extra work at all. At the page end the bar is usually hidden after scrolling down, so the 52 px gap is what you see most of the time.

Visual difference:
- Bar shown: same as dvh (panel 40 px shorter, fully visible)
- Bar hidden: panel 40 px shorter than today, 52 px empty band under it
- Nothing outside the panel changes

Option dvh: dvh fits in both bar states.
- Identical to today when the bar is hidden.
- Its extra cost lands only in frames where the panel moves anyway; a real-phone check confirms it before landing.
