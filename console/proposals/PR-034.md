## Problem

You asked to put the end of the page back to how fastwork.com does it, and keep everything above the end as in v14 (today's best build).
- Where you asked: your thread about the end of the page (T-228, messages 60 and 62).
- In v14 the menu at the end follows your finger, and the section before it ("One platform is everything you need") sticks while it opens.
- On fastwork.com the page stops at the end, and one swipe opens the menu by itself.
- Revision 2: on revision 1 you asked to add the fix for the Join waitlist button, which lifted up while it faded out as the end-of-page menu appeared.

## Idea

g0.0b is v14 with the original end of the page, plus the Join button fix you asked for. Everything above the end stays v14's.
A second step, g0.1 (the GPU step, by fix-g0-gpu), builds on it and moves the menu animation to the graphics chip to make it smoother.

## Looks the same?

Above the end, g0.0b shows the same pixels as v14 in Chrome at the four tested widths: the differences stay inside what two loads of v14 differ by.
At the end, it matches the original page except the Join button.
- Tested in Chrome at four widths (phone with and without the browser bar, tablet, desktop) and in the iPhone Simulator.
- At the closed menu, every 80 ms of the opening and closing, and with the menu open.
The Join button matches the original with only the lift removed: the look you accepted in PR-027 (your earlier proposal about the Join button), option "no lift".

## Risk

Not faster yet; the GPU step g0.1 follows to fix that.
- While the menu opens it redraws more per frame than v14, as on fastwork.com, so on a hot phone the opening can stutter like the original.
- The page holds still at the end: a swipe during the animation waits for it to finish. That is the original behaviour, but it can feel stiffer than v14's swipe-following menu.
- Not yet tried on the real iPhone with a finger; the checks used Chrome and the iPhone Simulator.

## Options

### Option g0: Original end of the page, with the Join button fix

The end of the page works as on fastwork.com again.
- The page holds still at the end; one swipe down opens the menu by itself over 680 ms, one swipe up closes it.
- The Join waitlist button moves aside as on the original, but no longer lifts up while it fades: it blurs and fades in place. The blur and the fade stay as in the option you accepted in PR-027 (your earlier proposal about the Join button).
- The section before the menu ("One platform is everything you need") scrolls normally again; v14 made it stick.
- The page is as long as the original: v14 added one extra screen of scroll after the end.

Risk: Not faster while the menu opens (see the shared risk); the GPU step g0.1 follows.

Visual difference:
- At the end: the menu opens by itself in 680 ms instead of following your finger (as on fastwork.com).
- The Join waitlist button no longer moves 8 pixels up while it is still visible.
- It blurs and fades in place: the look you accepted for the Join button (PR-027, option no lift).
- The same when it comes back as the menu closes.
- The section before the menu no longer sticks; it scrolls like the rest of the page (as on fastwork.com).
- Against the original page with that one fix: at most 54 colour levels of 255 differ, at the menu's glass edge; they come from v14 parts this step keeps.
- Above the end: no visible difference to v14 in Chrome; in the iPhone Simulator only the section before the menu, which no longer sticks.
