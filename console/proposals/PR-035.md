## Problem

A bug in the original site, which we can fix: in Safari the 2nd platform card shows grey squares in its corners.
- It is the card 'What do you need help with?'; the grey shows in all four rounded corners.
- The card has a blurred background picture under its content; Safari cuts it to the square box, not to the rounded corner.
- The other three cards have no blurred picture, so their corners are clean.
- A known Safari (WebKit) bug, already fixed in WebKit's newest code; iOS 26.5 still has it.
- Same on the original site, v14 and today's best build g0.0b; Chrome never shows it.

## Idea

One style line: cut the blurred picture to the card's own rounded shape.
- In Safari the grey squares go away; the corners then look like the other three cards.
- Nothing inside the card changes, and Chrome looks the same.
What you need to do: try A and B on the phone, look at the four corners of the second platform card, and pick one.

## Looks the same?

Everything inside the card and the rest of the page is pixel-identical (checked in the iOS Simulator and Chrome at phone, tablet and desktop widths).
- Only the corners outside the rounded edge, and the 1-pixel smoothed edge next to them, change.

## Risk

Low: one style line on one background picture.
- The Safari bug it works around is CONFIRMED: WebKit bug 312584, fixed in WebKit 313267@main.
- Once Safari ships that fix, the line simply does nothing extra.

## Visual difference

- Safari, phone size: the grey squares in the four corners of the second platform card are gone (navy, like the other cards).
- The card's 1-pixel smoothed edge is a touch crisper, like the other cards' edges; you would need to zoom in to see it.
