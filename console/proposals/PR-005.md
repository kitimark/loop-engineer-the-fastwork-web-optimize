## Problem

fwreskip is a scroll-engine change. When the hero leaves the screen, it also skips drawing the next section (#people) until you come near it again. In the Simulator this removes two layout steps of about 20 ms each, every time you scroll down past the hero.

The trade, measured on the phone in G1b (RB build, robot swipes, 8 runs each):
- Without the extra idle step, fwreskip made one 65-92 ms frame when a fast fling crossed #people. The current version (P2b) avoids that. When you stop in that area, it draws #people again after 0.3 s of rest.
- That idle step costs one 44-55 ms frame about 0.3 s after you stop. It was over 50 ms in 5 of 8 runs; today's frame there is 15-18 ms.
- The gain side did not show on the phone. The frame where the hero is skipped was 25-30 ms today and 25-33 ms with fwreskip. Over 50 ms happened in 1 of 8 runs in both.

What this rests on, plainly:
- Both sides of the trade come from G1b on the older base: RB, robot swipes (not your finger).
- The newer base (RB + avatar rotator st6) was checked only in the Simulator: counts, not phone times. It has the same two ~20 ms steps and the same idle step, nothing more and nothing cheaper.
- The planned phone test on the newer base, with a scripted scroll (G2), was not run because the phone is disconnected.
- Nothing here looks different: fwreskip only changes when a section is drawn. Screens are byte-identical in Chrome (P2 build; P2b adds only teardown).

## Options

### Option ship-p2b: A: ship fwreskip (P2b)

Land fwreskip P2b on the current stack. The hero and #people skip drawing together, and #people is drawn again 0.3 s after you stop near it.

Risk: It adds a frame of about 50 ms that you don't have today, and no gain was seen on the phone. Not checked: your finger, the newer base on the phone, and whether the phone's display process shows an empty #people during a fast fling (no screen recordings since T-018).

Visual difference:
- Nothing visible in the page itself. What you might notice: a short hitch in whatever animates on screen about 0.3 s after you stop scrolling in the #people area.
