## Problem

Answer: half of all incidents (72 of 145) are team slips, not page bugs, and most come from agents copying facts the tools already have.

- The board learns about your answers late: 7 of your 19 answers today took over 10 min to show (median 9 min; the decision on the hero accent word proposal took 17 min, I-145).
- The lead missed that decision too: its listener filter drops proposal lines.
- Agents write about 575 board updates a day, roughly 5 % of one usage window (estimate).
- The lead writes about half of all output tokens, mostly coordination.
- 9 agents answered 'what slowed you today': hand-made chains of lock holds, reading each other's files, phone hand-offs, and copied scripts.

Full plan: research/architecture/team-ops-plan.md.

What you need to do: pick one option.

## Risk

All options change tools only through toolsmiths, one incident at a time, with tests. Nothing changes how pages are measured.

## Options

### Option tools-first: Tools first, no new role

- Board: once you answer, 'waiting on you' disappears at once (from the event, not from the agent).
- One wake line for every event, so no listener can filter a decision out.
- Lock: run a chain of holds; reserve the phone for a promised user step.
- A read-only peer file reader; flaky-test reports joined, not filed four times.
- The desk answers status questions on a cheaper model; decisions still go to the lead.

Risk: Medium for the lock changes (chain, phone reservation): fairness needs tests. Low for the rest (read-only views).

Visual difference:
- Board rows show 'you answered at 13:09Z; owner has not picked it up' in place of a stale 'waiting on you'

### Option tools-and-check: Tools + a team check every ~6 h

- Everything in 'Tools first'.
- Agents log friction in one line when it happens (no extra turn).
- A short-lived checker on a cheaper model, every ~6 h or after each wave, reads that log, asks only the agents with no note, and hands the lead ready incident lines.
- It also re-measures board lag, lead load and minutes you waited on a stuck step, so you can see the trend.

Risk: Costs a little every round; agents may ignore the friction log (the checker then asks them).

Visual difference:
- One short FYI thread per check (every ~6 h) with what it found

| Extra cost | - | ~2-4 M units/day (~2-3 % of one window) | estimate | ~10 agent turns per round plus the checker |

- One wake line for every event, so the lead never misses a decision.
- The router ignores agents whose listener stopped (I-137).
- Everything else stays as today.

Risk: Lowest. Lock chains, phone hand-offs and double work stay as they are.
