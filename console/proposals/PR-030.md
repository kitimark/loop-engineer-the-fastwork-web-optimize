# PR-030: End of the page: the menu card opens on the graphics chip (a third of the painting), looks slightly different

## Problem

At the end of the page the menu card grows from the top bar into a full card as you scroll.
- Today the phone redraws the card and works out its size again on almost every frame of that grow.
- On the iPhone that costs about 12 paints and 2.3 size calculations (layouts) per frame. This is the end-of-page lag you flagged as the top priority.

## Idea

The card is drawn once in its final size, and the scroll moves it on the graphics chip.
- Four nested clipping boxes cut today's shape out of it, scrubbed by the scroll like a video.
- The page code no longer resizes the card on every frame.
- Only phones get it (touch screens up to 640 px wide, motion not reduced). Tablets, desktop and Safari on the Mac keep today's version.

In Try-it, A is today's best build (v9-final) and B is v9-final plus this change.

## Looks the same?

The card's size, corners, colours, blur and timing follow today's at every scroll position. The differences are small and listed below.

## Risk

It changes how the card is drawn, so it needs your eye, not only numbers.
- The look differences above are small but real, mostly while the card is moving.
- It uses about 14 more graphics layers while the card is open.
- The phone's frame times did not get clearly better at this heat level; the gain is less work per frame, which matters most when the phone is hot.
- The phone still shows a few slow frames right at the end, fully open: 3-6 frames over 50 ms per run in a later check. Finding their cause is the next step.

## Visual difference

- Early in the grow (first 40 %): the top bar's left part looks slightly different.
- The logo and the bar's edge: 0.6-3 % of the screen, by small amounts.
- From 80 % of the grow on: the whole card's content sits up to about 1 px off.
- The blurred photos behind it differ a little too.
- At 80 %: 3.2 % of pixels differ by more than a faint shade, 0.93 % clearly, 0.32 % strongly.
- Fully open and at rest: the same as today on the iPhone engine (0.07-0.11 %).
- In Chrome (Android) the card's text can look a little softer after a slow scroll to the end: 2 % of pixels, on text edges.
- After a quick jump to the end it is 0.2 %.
- Known issue, Chrome screenshots only: the card's bottom corners can show as see-through.
- What the screen actually shows is clean there, and so is the iPhone engine.
