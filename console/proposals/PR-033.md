## Problem

You saw the logo, the language button and the Join button move near the end of the page. We found why.
- On today's best build (v14), the whole top row rises 4 px while the menu card opens, evenly from closed to fully open.
- The original site does the same: its style rule moves the bar from 16 px to 12 px from the top as the card grows.
- Nothing in the row moves before the card starts to open.
- UNRESOLVED (not checked yet): Safari's bottom toolbar collapsing during a finger drag also changes the screen height mid-drag. That may be part of what you saw; our checks use a fixed screen height.

## Idea

Three options, all on today's best build (v14). Only the row's height on screen changes; the card, the menu and the timing stay as today.
- Keep: as today, the row rises 4 px while the card opens.
- Hold (recommended): the row never moves. At fully open it sits 4 px lower than today.
- Late rise: the row stays still until the card is 60 % open, then rises its 4 px; fully open looks exactly like today.
- Speed: no change expected (one value in the same animation); not measured.
What you need to do: try the options on the phone and pick one.
