# PR-023: End-of-page menu: a long swipe back up leaves the end in one go (rev 7d, phones)

## Problem

With the accepted menu fix, a long swipe back up from the open menu stops at the closed menu.
- Accepted fix: the end-of-page menu fix you accepted (PR-018 rev 7c).
- You need a second swipe to leave the end of the page.
- On the best build without that fix (v8) the same swipe carries on up the page.
- Our combined test build (v9 step 4) showed this as a regression.

## Idea

One extra rule when your finger lifts: a long or fast swipe back up keeps its own momentum.
- Long: your finger moved 300 px or more. Fast: 5 px per ms or more.
- It then scrolls on like a normal page. If it slows down while still at the end, it settles to closed as before.
- Opening, slow swipes and short closes behave exactly like rev 7c.
- Your own recorded closes were 74-255 px long and at most about 4 px per ms, so both lines sit above them.
- Builds (rev 5, on the final v9 as you asked): A = final v9 as it ships (with rev 7c); B = the same final v9 with rev 7d.
- A and B differ only in this lift-off rule (the same 15 lines as from rev 7c to rev 7d).
- Both have the nav colour fix; the menu-close repaint fix was left out of v9 (planned for v9.1). Heat mode (Serious) is on.

## Looks the same?

Yes in still frames; only the motion of long or fast swipes up from the open menu differs.
- The change is in the rule that runs when your finger lifts.
- The styles are byte-identical in A and B (final v9 CSS file e1359ab9994a).

## Risk

A motion change: you decide whether a long swipe up from the open menu should leave the end in one go.
- On this iPhone only the length rule (300 px) let the robot swipes go; the speed rule never fired.
- The page read at most 3.6 px per ms on a 560 px swipe in 0.14 s, under the 5 px per ms line (in our setup, cause unknown).
- Margin: your closes so far were at most 255 px; a robot swipe of 280 px still stopped at the closed menu.
- So a close of about 300 px or more is now let go, even a slow one if it is long.
- One robot run per build for each check: counts, not rates.
- Unchanged on both builds, cause still unknown: now and then a swipe up out of the end glides away and comes back to open.
- Not run yet: your own finger on this build.
Why A and B both felt laggy to you (rev 3, your change request):
- Your phone was in Xcode's heat mode (Serious) during your try: read at 04:28, right after it. That slows every page on purpose.
- The lag is shared: A and B differ only in long swipes up. In our robot runs both had the same slow frames, most of them above the menu.
- Where you felt it, after letting go: in your own swipes on A (4 Oct, normal heat), 13 of 21 slow frames came 0.19-0.22 s after the lift, one or two missed frames each (cause not known yet).
- Making it smooth in heat mode is a research job (the heat-mode agent); a fix would come to you as its own proposal.
- Update: the menu-close repaint fix was left out of the final v9; it is planned for v9.1.
- Workaround now: stop the heat mode (step C-055) to feel the page at normal heat. Choosing A or B does not depend on it.

## Visual difference

- Motion only: from the open menu, a long or fast swipe back up keeps scrolling up out of the end screen (B). In A it stops at the closed menu.
- Short closes, slow lifts and opens: identical in A and B (robot checks below).
- Still frames: identical (same styles; the change is in the lift-off rule only).
- Nothing changes on desktop, tablets or with reduced motion.
- The chart and the robot counts below come from the v8 builds; on v9 the rule's code is the same.
- The chart: across, 0-3 s after the finger lands (a tick every 0.5 s); down, the page position. Dashed top line = end of page, grey line = closed menu.
