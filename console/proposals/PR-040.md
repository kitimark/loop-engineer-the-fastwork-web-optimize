# PR-040: End of page: the menu follows your finger again, with a steady glass top bar (no flicker)

## Problem

You asked for the end-of-page menu that grows with your swipe, without the glass flicker.
- In the earlier glass-menu proposal (PR-030), the top bar's frosted glass vanished in many frames while your finger moved near the start of the grow.
- iPhone Simulator, finger wiggling there: glass missing in 12 of 33 frames; EN and Join changed too.
- Our older build v14 (the last one where the menu followed the finger) had two more glitches:
- after scrolling into the menu and back, the logo jumped 20 px left and the buttons 4 px right at every crossing;
- the Join button's rainbow sweep (its one-time intro) played again each time you stopped near the end or left it.

## Idea

Bring back v14's end of the page (the menu grows with your swipe) on top of today's best build g0.0h, and fix all three glitches.
- Glass: the bar keeps its normal blur the whole time; only its colour darkens as the menu grows, the way the original page changes it.
- Nothing animates the blur any more, so Safari has nothing to drop.
- This replaces v13/v14's per-frame blur change (20 px fading to 0 while the menu grows).
- Logo and buttons keep their place after you scroll back.
- The Join sweep plays once when the bar appears, as in g0.0h, and never replays at the end.
- Above the end of the page the code is exactly g0.0h's.

## Looks the same?

Above the end of the page: the same as g0.0h (same code).
- While the menu grows: the earlier glass menu's look (PR-030, the proposal you accepted before), with the glass kept frosted.

## Risk

Medium: it changes how the end of the page behaves.
- It reverses your end-of-page rollback (T-228 #62, the menu opening by itself at the end): here the menu grows with your swipe, as in v14.
- Checked on the iPhone Simulator and Chrome only; not yet on the real iPhone or with your finger.
- It keeps v14's always-on mouse-wheel listener (a script that watches wheel and trackpad scrolling); Safari does a bit more style work once at page load.
- Near the end, menu closed, it keeps 7 more layers (pictures the screen keeps) and about 26 MB more graphics memory than g0.0h, so opening costs nothing.
- That is still fewer than the earlier glass menu (PR-030) and v14.

## Visual difference

- While the menu grows, the earlier glass menu's known differences stay (PR-030, the proposal you accepted before):
- early in the grow the bar's top-left (logo, edge) looks slightly different;
- near fully open, content can sit up to ~1 px off.
- The glass keeps its blur while the menu grows (v14 let it fade).
- In the Simulator the frames match v14's at the same point of the grow (0.04 % of pixels at the start, 0 a third of the way in).
- Chrome only:
- after a slow approach the fully open menu's text can stay a little soft;
- Chrome screenshots (not the screen) can show see-through card corners.
- iPhone Simulator only, above the end:
- in the platform section, text and pictures sit a fraction of a screen pixel off from g0.0h at 2 of 7 places;
- probably because this page is 815 px longer; Chrome showed none.
