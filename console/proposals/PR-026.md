## Problem

Where and when: the countdown clock inside the end-of-page menu, while the menu is open at the bottom of the page. The page rests there with the menu open.
- Each second the clock ticks and its digits roll for about half a second.
- Today they roll by a script that moves them every frame (a spring): about 27 animation callbacks per second at rest, and every one of them makes the browser re-style and repaint.
- The top clock already rolls its digits with browser animations instead (your accepted roll, 'K'). The menu clock was left on the old spring.
Numbers below: our counts only, no timing.

## Idea

Give the menu clock the same roll as the top clock: the browser moves the digits itself, the script only starts each roll.
- Only while the menu is fully open or fully closed. While the menu opens or closes (it changes size), the clock keeps the old spring, so a roll never runs during the resize.
- A roll that is running when the menu starts to move is handed to the spring at its current position (no jump: at most 0.001 px in 12 of 12 checks).
- About 60 changed lines of script; no style change; the top clock's code is not touched.

## Looks the same?

At rest (between rolls) and with reduced motion: the same pixels.
- Safari (iPhone Simulator): exact in 24 of 24 frozen frames, the same as two loads of today's page.
- Chrome: 10 pixels on two card corners are off by 1 of 255, in exactly the same way when today's page is loaded twice.
- Chrome while the digits roll: at most 1 of 255 against today, at the same frozen moment.
- While the menu opens or closes: nothing changes (same spring, same paint work per frame).

## Risk

Low to medium.
- The difference while digits roll in Safari (above) is real in the Simulator; the iPhone itself is not checked yet.
- Measured on v9-final before the menu change rev 7d. Try it is on today's v9-final (with rev 7d): the change applies cleanly there and touches none of rev 7d's lines.
- Not checked with the next build in the works (v9.1d), which hides the closed menu's parts for 0.75 s after it closes.

## Visual difference

- Safari only, while a digit rolls (about 0.5 s each second, menu open): the moving digits' edges look a bit different.
- Frozen 133 ms into a roll: edges up to 92 of 255 lighter or darker.
- 867 device pixels, about 96 CSS px², 0.024 % of the screen, same in all 6 checks.
- Gone when the roll ends.
- Likely cause (from a note in the page code, not checked): during a roll the digits sit on their own drawing layer, as on the top clock.
- Safari smooths letter edges differently on such a layer.
- In Chrome the same moment differs by at most 1 of 255, so the digits follow the same path.
- Seen in the iPhone Simulator only.
- The real iPhone is not checked yet: a short phone check that nothing gets worse comes next, only if you accept.
- A difference you can see only in motion, so it is your call.
- Open Try it, scroll to the bottom so the menu is open, and watch the menu clock for a few seconds.
