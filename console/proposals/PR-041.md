## Problem

A fast flick down from the top makes the profile deck blink: white, then blurred, then sharp.
- You saw it on the deck with the photos; the original site has it too.
- Cause (observed on the iPhone, in the Simulator and in Chrome; our tests, not a browser document): the card fades in over time.
- The fade is opacity 0.7 s and blur 0.9 s in today's best build g0.0k (1.6 s blur in the original).
- It starts at a fixed point below the hero; a fast flick brings the deck into view ~0.3 s later, mid-fade.
- iPhone, 4 robot flicks per build: the blur ends at the landing in the original, ~0.8 s before it in g0.0j.
- The screen kept 60 frames a second throughout: it is not a freeze.
- With the fade switched off for a test the deck is sharp in every frame; switching off the deck's own effects changes nothing.

## Looks the same?

Slow scrolling, a page at rest and Reduce Motion keep today's look; only the fast case changes.
- Simulator, slow drags (0.47-0.57 px per ms): nothing triggers; the card fades exactly as in g0.0k.
- At rest with the deck in view, the only differences from g0.0k are things that move by themselves.
- Those are the clock, the spinning record and the avatar row.
- Plus 40 device pixels at the profile photo's edge, up to 31 of 255: within the agreed look-alike numbers.
- Scrolling back up: the card hides with today's fade-out.

## Risk

Low: about 30 lines of script and one CSS rule on g0.0k, inside the people section's existing scroll code.
- 'Fast' means at least 0.8 px per ms in the frame where the card would start fading. Our robot flicks on the iPhone measured 1.0-2.9; a slow drag is about 0.5. A brisk but not flicked scroll may also count as fast and get the new look.
- The two deck profiles you see first (their photos and cover) now load and decode about 1.5 s after the page settles, on every visit. Today they load only when you scroll near: about 15 more images up front.
- Not yet run on the real iPhone; checked in the iPhone Simulator (Safari's engine). Chrome runs the same code path.

## Options

### Option instant: Fast scroll: the card appears at once, already sharp

On a fast scroll the card skips its fade and shows its final look at once.
- No blur, no fade, no lift; slower scrolls still get today's fade.
- The deck's first two profiles load their photos early (after the page settles), so nothing pops in on a deck you can now see.

Risk: The card's appearance is sudden on fast scrolls (that is the change).

Visual difference:
- Fast flick: the card is sharp from its first frame on screen.
- Simulator sharpness 13-14, as at rest; today 1.6-3.8 until the deck is about halfway in.
- The moment the card appears is the same as today (the same scroll point); it just has no fade.
- Slow scroll, rest, Reduce Motion, scrolling up: as today.

### Option quick: Fast scroll: a short 0.15 s fade instead

The same switch, but on a fast scroll the card fades in over 0.15 s instead of 0.7-0.9 s.
- It is sharp before the deck arrives; slower scrolls get today's fade; same early photo loading.

Risk: Same as the first option. On a very fast flick the 0.15 s fade can still overlap the deck's first frames.

Visual difference:
- Fast flick: a brief fade when the card appears, mostly while the hero is still on screen.
- The deck is sharp when it comes into view (Simulator sharpness 12.4-14).
- Slow scroll, rest, Reduce Motion, scrolling up: as today.
