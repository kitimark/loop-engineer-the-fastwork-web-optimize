## Problem

Four small scripts keep working while nothing on screen changes. The worst is the 'people' avatar row: it starts a slide every second, even when the row is far off screen, so the page restyles and repaints that row about 30 times in 4 seconds for nothing. The rest-script change (v4) stops that work when the row can't be seen. The change has to look the same as today. It does in most places, within the agreed wide numbers (PR-010 T2). But with reduced motion turned on, it is not exact, and your rule says reduced motion must be exact. So by the rules this is a T3 result, and it needs your decision.

## Looks the same?

With normal motion it looks the same within the PR-010 wide numbers. Chrome at phone widths (375, 430) is identical. Chrome 1024, the iPhone Simulator and the iPad Simulator differ only on avatar outlines: at most 97 of 255 shades, over at most 427 CSS px, and at most 0.09 % of the screen (the largest values over all shots, not from one shot). The limits are fill 64 / edge 128 shades, 2000 CSS px and 0.5 %. With reduced motion it is NOT exact in Chrome at 430: every still shot differs evenly by at most 4 shades. Chrome 1440 (desktop) has no scored result for the row scrolled into view.

## Options

### Option keep: Keep the change

Keep rest-script v4. It would land only after the lead un-parks it and a short iPhone no-worse check passes (the less-work landing rule); that check has not run yet, and it would queue after the current phone tests. Once landed, the row stops sliding while it is off screen, and the counters stop rewriting values that don't change. On screen it slides as before.

Risk: Reduced-motion users get a still avatar row that is not pixel-exact (up to 4 shades, cause unknown) and none of the saving: with reduced motion on, no work is saved (census 430r: all SAME). Desktop 1440 has no scored result for the scrolled-into-view row. In the iPhone Simulator the row still differs (T2) at the rest shot, about 1.4 s after a slide ends, and is identical 2.5 s after (OBSERVED, cause unknown); in the iPad Simulator the rest shot is already identical. Landing still needs the lead's un-park and a phone no-worse check.
