## Problem

On desktop, two cards in the middle of the page move by themselves after you scroll (the 'double drive' research you asked to catch up on, thread T-157):
- The future card in the 'people' section shrinks with a 0.9 s CSS animation that restarts every frame, so it lags 0.7-1.3 s behind the scroll.
- Scroll up fast and that card jumps by up to 270 px in one frame, because its shrink anchor flips while it is still shrunk.
- The commission card jumps about 31 px in one frame when you scroll back up, for the same reason.

## Idea

The page's script already smooths these cards' shrink every frame, so the CSS animation on top is removed while the script drives them.
- Future card: its shrink anchor stays at the bottom, and its 'shrinking' state is held until the card is back at full size.
- Commission card: its anchor moves over a few frames instead of flipping in one.
- At rest every card sits exactly where it does today.

Builds: A = v8 (the best build), B = v8 + this fix. The same fix is part 3 of the next best build (v9).

## Looks the same?

Yes, at rest. Still frames match v8:
- Chrome desktop, 19 positions: equal apart from the fee counter's count-up (it differs between two loads of v8 too) and 2 css px² at one spot.
- Chrome phone, 13 positions: the same.
- Safari (iPhone Simulator), 11 positions: equal apart from the hero animation, which also differs between two loads of v8.
Only the motion while scrolling changes: no trail and no jump.

## Risk

- Desktop motion only: you decide whether the cards should follow the scroll without their own trail.
- Fast scroll up: the future card keeps its 'shrinking' look a few frames longer, until it is back at full size.
- Not yet run on the real iPhone (no-worse check drafted). No timing numbers: timing is deferred.
- Not touched: the menu and the end-of-page panel (another fix's area).

## Visual difference

- Desktop, while scrolling: the future card's size follows your scroll at once (no 0.7-1.3 s trail).
- Desktop, fast scroll up: no card jump (was up to 270 px on the future card and 31 px on the commission card).
- Phones: no visible change. The commission card does not shrink there, and the future card's scale stays 1. Only an invisible restarting animation is removed.
